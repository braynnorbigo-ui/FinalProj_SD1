BookWebCATC

BookWebCATC is an integrated library catalog, physical book borrowing desk, and student library card portal crafted for CAT College. Designed to connect walk-in library operations with digital tracking, it helps librarians manage book availability, physical returns, and official student library cards while giving students a clear view of the library catalog and their member profile.

Smart Catalog & Search
Browse department books, shelf availability, accession codes, and ISBNs.
Walk-In Borrowing & Returns
Record physical checkouts, return schedules, and instant shelf status updates.
Official Library Cards
Maintain registered student accounts and preview/print CATC library cards.
